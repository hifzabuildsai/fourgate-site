Fourgate catches MCP tools that say "success" when nothing actually happened. It is early, pre-alpha software. These pages describe **v0.3.0** and nothing newer.

After a connector reports success, Fourgate extracts only the fields a contract approves, runs a deterministic verifier against the system of record, and records one of three outcomes: <V s="PASS" />, <V s="FAIL" /> or <V s="UNKNOWN" />. No LLM makes that decision.

## Where to start

- [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart): install, run `fourgate demo`, and read the three verdicts.
- [Concepts](/docs/concepts): outcome contracts, read-back verification, shadow and enforce mode, failure behavior, and why UNKNOWN is never PASS.
- [Protect your first tool](/docs/guides/first-tool): `init`, `doctor`, `scan`, `guard`, `summary`, in that order.
- [Reference](/docs/reference/commands): commands and flags, contract JSON, the outcome log, and exit codes.
- [Security summary](/docs/security): what runs where, what is stored, and what leaves your machine.

## What v0.3.0 covers

- Command-configured local stdio MCP servers only. Remote (HTTP) MCP servers are not supported yet.
- Contracts are written by hand and approved by a person. Generating them is not implemented.
- The bundled verifier, `fourgate.verify_http`, is a generic HTTP GET read-back. There are no database adapters.
- There is no dashboard, alerting product, gateway, retry engine or automatic compensation.
